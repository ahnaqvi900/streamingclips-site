Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how StreamingClips ("the app", "we") handles information when it connects to TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google).

1. Who uses the app

StreamingClips is operated by a single individual to publish short videos to accounts that the operator owns. It is not offered to the public, and no outside user can sign up.

2. Information we access

When the operator connects a social media account, the app receives, through the platform's official sign-in (OAuth):

  • basic profile information, such as the account's display name, user ID and avatar;
  • an access token that allows the app to upload and publish videos to that account;
  • the publishing status of videos the app has uploaded.

The app does not request passwords, and it does not read private messages, contacts, followers' personal data or payment information.

3. How we use it

Information is used only to upload and publish videos to the connected account, to check whether a post succeeded, and to show the operator which account is connected.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored by our integration provider (Composio) in encrypted form and are used only for the purposes above. Video files are stored temporarily for processing and deleted after they are published.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, except with the service providers needed to run the app (such as Composio and cloud hosting) and with the platforms themselves when publishing.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while an account remains connected. Disconnecting the app, either from the platform's settings (for example TikTok: Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or by contacting us, revokes access, and the related tokens are deleted.

7. Third-party platforms

Use of TikTok, Instagram and YouTube is also governed by their own policies, including the TikTok Privacy Policy, Meta Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of YouTube API Services follows the YouTube Terms of Service.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
