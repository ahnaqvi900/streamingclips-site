StreamingClips

StreamingClips is a personal content tool that turns highlights from live streams into short vertical videos and publishes them to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

The tool only posts to accounts owned by its operator. It does not offer accounts to the public, and it does not collect data from other users.
